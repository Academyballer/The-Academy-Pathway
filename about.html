About Me

Welcome, my fellow ambitious player.

Do you want to make it into an academy like I did? Then this website is for you.

Every player's journey is different. Here is mine.

I started playing football at my local club when I was five years old. I was a good player, but I wasn't one of the best.

I didn't have anyone to guide me. I simply played football because I loved it.

When I turned eleven, everything changed. I started training with a real purpose. Every session had a goal, and every day I wanted to improve.

Two years later, at the age of thirteen, I signed for Malmö FF.

Everything I learned on my journey is now here to help you create yours.
