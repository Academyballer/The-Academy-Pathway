About Me

Welcome, my fellow ambitious player.

Do you want to make it into an academy like I did? Then this website is for you.

Every journey is different, and here is mine.

I started playing for my local club when I was five years old. I was good, but I wasn't one of the best.

I didn't have anyone to guide me, and I mostly played football for fun until I was eleven.

At eleven, I started training the right way. At the age of thirteen, I signed for Malmö FF.

Everything I learned along the way is now here to help you follow your own pathway.
